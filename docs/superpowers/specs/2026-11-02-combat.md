# Combat simulation and feedback

Continue the authorized asset/animation/gameplay work with deterministic simulation separated from Three.js rendering. Keep local file and localhost launch, responsive controls, saves and earned progression.

Combat advances on fixed 1/60-second ticks with seeded critical rolls. Each equipped weapon supplies its share of total displayed DPS and has its own cooldown: iron cleave, poison crescent, two-target armor fracture, ordinary-enemy stun, fire splash/burn, frost slow. DOT applications retain independent tick clocks and expire without losing pending damage on refresh. Death emits one event; rendering applies rewards and respawn resets attack/status state.

Enemies independently approach, wind up at locked ground targets, strike and recover. Boss circles are larger, every third slam expands further, and low health accelerates attacks/damage. Movement can dodge after windup begins. Bosses resist stun. Pause freezes simulation and effects.

Rendering consumes weapon/damage/status/strike/death events; champion attack playback is throttled to finish its sequence. Creature colors show status; windups use the attack pose. Colored impacts and ground telegraphs use a fixed 144-particle, 12-ring pool plus one marker per fixed enemy. Weapon audio uses distinct pitches/timbres while respecting mute.

Verification includes deterministic frame subdivisions, independent cooldowns/attacks, full continuous DOT budgets, stun/slow, boss enrage/dodge, death once and reset, pause, six effects in actual browser play, keyboard dodging, and repeated FX pool reuse.
